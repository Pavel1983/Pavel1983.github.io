About me

I’ve spent my career making games. I started in C++ on PC titles like Farm Frenzy and Roads of Rome 2, then moved to Unity and mobile — leading development on hyper-casual games and later working on two of the largest projects at Tripledot Studios.

Today I also run my own small studio: I design, code and polish every app myself, from the first sketch to the release. My idea is simple — small things done carefully: clear rules, gentle difficulty, no clutter, and respect for players’ privacy and time.
