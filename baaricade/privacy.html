Privacy Policy — Ba-arricade

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This is the full privacy notice for Ba-arricade, a puzzle game for Android in which you place signs, feeders, and a dog to steer rams so the farmer can fence off the field. This is the URL we use on the Google Play store listing. The site is served over HTTPS.

This notice describes what the game stores on your device, what data the advertising SDK collects, how the in-app purchase works, what anonymous gameplay statistics the game sends, how we measure our own ad campaigns, which third parties receive data, and what choices you have.

Who we are

Ba-arricade is made by Pavel Totolin, an independent developer. This notice applies to anyone who plays the game, wherever they are.

The game has no account or login. We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number, and we do not run our own servers for the game.

What the game stores on your device

The game keeps its progress locally on the device:

  • the level you have reached
  • which tutorial tips and new-element cards you have already seen
  • how many free hints you have used today
  • how many levels you have won and how many ads between levels have been shown (used to decide when the next ad between levels is due)
  • whether you bought “Remove Ads”, and when the offer to remove ads was last shown
  • whether you accepted this policy, and the date of the first launch
  • your settings: music and sound volume, language, and whether gameplay statistics are allowed

This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads: a banner at the bottom of the screen, ads between levels (interstitial ads, shown now and then after a level is won), and optional rewarded videos that you can choose to watch for a hint once the day’s free hints are used up. After the “Remove Ads” purchase there is no banner and no ads between levels; rewarded videos stay available if you choose to watch them. Ads are served through the Appodeal mediation SDK. Appodeal requests ads from its demand partners and ad networks (for example Google AdMob, AppLovin, Unity Ads, ironSource, Meta Audience Network, Mintegral, Liftoff (Vungle), BidMachine, Yandex, and others) and shows the one that wins.

What the advertising SDK collects

To request, show, and measure ads, Appodeal and its partners may collect:

  • the device’s advertising ID (Google Advertising ID on Android)
  • the device’s IP address
  • device and app information: device model and manufacturer, operating system and version, language, screen size, network type and carrier, app name and version, time zone
  • a coarse location (country or region) derived from the IP address
  • ad interactions: which ads were requested, shown, clicked, or watched to the end, and ad revenue

This data is used to deliver ads, limit how often the same ad is shown, measure ad performance and attribution, prevent fraud, and — if you allow it — show personalized (interest-based) ads.

Appodeal’s SDK also includes optional measurement and diagnostics services (such as Adjust, AppsFlyer, Meta, and Sentry). When they are active, they receive similar technical data about the device, the app, and ad events. Separately, the game itself uses Google Analytics for Firebase to measure our own ad campaigns — see “Ad campaign measurement” below.

Location and permissions

The game does not request location permission and does not collect precise GPS location. A coarse region may be inferred from the IP address by Appodeal or ad networks. The game does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, or photos.

Consent in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the game shows a consent message (Google’s consent form, provided through Appodeal) before personalized ads. You can accept or refuse. If you refuse, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized, and ad partners are told not to use your data for profiling.

You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices opens the same consent form again. Withdrawing consent does not affect ads you have already seen, and your game progress is kept. You can also contact us by email.

Your advertising choices on Android

On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the device). After that, ad networks receive no advertising ID or are told not to personalize ads.

Game engine

Ba-arricade is built with the Unity engine. The engine may send anonymous technical data to Unity, such as device model, operating system, graphics hardware, and performance or crash information, to help Unity improve the engine. Unity’s practices: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

In-app purchases

The game offers one optional purchase: “Remove Ads”, a one-time purchase that turns off the banner and the ads between levels. Payment is processed by Google Play. We never see or receive your card or bank details.

After a purchase, the store gives the game an order record: the product, an order ID, and a receipt signed by the store. The game checks the receipt’s signature on your device and saves on the device that ads are removed. When the game starts, it asks the store which one-time purchases your store account owns, so “Remove Ads” comes back after a reinstall or on a new device (there is also a “Restore” button in Settings). The purchase is handled through Unity’s in-app purchasing library, which runs inside the game. We do not run a server that receives your purchases. If gameplay statistics are allowed (see below), the fact of the purchase — product, price, and currency — may be included in them, both in Unity Analytics and in Google Analytics for Firebase.

Google Play’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Gameplay statistics (Unity Analytics)

To balance the difficulty of the levels, find problems, and understand which parts of the game are used, the game sends anonymous gameplay statistics to Unity Analytics, part of Unity Gaming Services. Statistics are sent only after you accept this policy in the game, and only while the statistics switch in Settings → Privacy is on. In the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the game working well (see “Legal bases” below); you can object at any time by turning the switch off.

The statistics are linked to the same anonymous player ID described in “Game settings from Unity Gaming Services”, not to your name, email, or advertising ID. They include:

  • game events: levels started; the result of each attempt (won or lost, how much of the field was fenced, on which step, how many items were placed, how many hints were shown, time spent); hints used and whether they were free or for a video; your language and volume settings; and whether an offer to remove ads was shown, closed, or used
  • ad events: which kind of ad was shown or offered (banner, ad between levels, rewarded video) and the ad revenue reported by Appodeal
  • purchase events: the product, price, and currency (never payment details)
  • device and app information: device model, operating system and version, language, app version, and a coarse country or region derived from the IP address

We use these Unity statistics only to improve the game. We do not use them for advertising, we do not pass them to ad networks, and we do not sell them. (A smaller, separate set of events goes to Google Analytics for Firebase to measure our ad campaigns; it is described in the next section.)

You can turn gameplay statistics off at any time in Settings → Privacy. In the same place, Delete my data asks Unity to delete the statistics already collected for your installation (the same button also resets the Firebase data described below). Unity keeps the statistics according to its own retention rules: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Ad campaign measurement (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We may advertise Ba-arricade, for example with Google Ads. To see which of our campaigns bring players who enjoy the game, and to let Google Ads optimize them, the game sends a small set of events to Google Analytics for Firebase, a service run by Google. Like the gameplay statistics, it is turned on only after you accept this policy in the game and only while the statistics switch in Settings → Privacy is on; turning the switch off stops it.

Google Analytics for Firebase receives:

  • game events: levels started and finished (the level, whether it was won, and the attempt number), milestones such as “10 levels completed”, the tutorial completed, and rewarded videos watched to the end
  • ad events: each ad shown, its format, the ad network, and the ad revenue reported by Appodeal
  • purchase events: the “Remove Ads” purchase, its price, and currency (recorded by Firebase from Google Play; never payment details)
  • player properties: how many levels have been completed and whether “Remove Ads” was bought
  • events Firebase records by itself, such as the first launch, sessions, and app updates
  • identifiers: a random app instance ID created by Firebase for your installation and, if available, the device’s advertising ID
  • device and app information: device model, operating system and version, language, app version, and a coarse country or region derived from the IP address

Google uses this data to provide the analytics to us and, through Google Ads, to attribute installs and in-game events to our ad campaigns and to optimize those campaigns. In the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, using the data for ads (ad storage, ad user data, and ad personalization) follows the choice you make in Google’s consent form; if you refuse, the data is used for analytics only. We do not sell this data.

For players who are signed in to a Google account and have allowed ads personalization in it, Google may associate these statistics with that account. This gives us aggregated reports on age, gender, and interests and lets us build audiences for our Google Ads campaigns; we never see who you are. You control this in your Google account: My Ad Center (turn off personalized ads) and activity controls.

Settings → Privacy → Delete my data stops the collection and resets the Firebase app instance ID and the data still stored on the device. Google’s practices: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Game settings from Unity Gaming Services

When the game starts, it downloads its settings (for example, how often ads are shown and how many free hints there are per day) from Unity Gaming Services, a service run by Unity Technologies. To do this, Unity creates an anonymous player ID for your installation; gameplay statistics use the same ID. It is a random identifier that is not linked to your name, email, or any account. With each request Unity also sees basic device information: device model, operating system and version, app version, language, and the IP address (from which a coarse country or region may be derived), as well as the date of the first launch, the level you have reached, and whether “Remove Ads” was bought, so that settings can differ for new and returning players. Unity uses this data only to deliver the right settings to the game and to keep the service running. Unity’s practices: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

What we do not collect

  • name, email, phone number, or login
  • contacts, photos, camera, or microphone
  • precise GPS location
  • payment card or bank details (purchases are processed by Google Play)

Legal bases (EEA, UK, and Switzerland)

Under the GDPR and similar laws we rely on these legal bases:

  • Consent — personalized (interest-based) ads and the related ad measurement, including the use of Google Analytics for Firebase data for Google Ads and its association with Google accounts. You give or refuse it in Google’s consent form and can change it in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices.
  • Legitimate interests — showing non-personalized ads to fund a free game, limiting how often ads appear, preventing ad fraud, gameplay statistics to balance and fix the game, analytics of our own ad campaigns in Google Analytics for Firebase, and downloading game settings. You can object: turn off the statistics switch, refuse personalized ads, or write to us.
  • Performance of a contract — processing the “Remove Ads” purchase and restoring it on your devices.

Data stored only on your device (your progress and settings) is used solely to provide the game you asked for.

Who receives data

  • ad networks and demand partners working through Appodeal, including Google AdMob — partner list
  • Unity (game engine, Unity Gaming Services, and Unity Analytics) — privacy policy
  • Google (Google Analytics for Firebase and Google Ads, to measure our ad campaigns) — privacy policy

These parties process data under their own policies. This notice does not replace their policies. We do not sell personal data for money. Sharing data with ad partners for interest-based advertising may count as “sharing” or “selling” under some laws (for example the California Consumer Privacy Act); you can stop it with the Android advertising settings described above.

Children

Ba-arricade is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has used the game and data was collected, contact us and we will help.

International processing

Appodeal, ad networks, Unity, and Google may process data on servers in countries other than the one where you play, including the United States. Some ad partners that can take part in ad auctions are based in countries without an EU adequacy decision — for example Yandex (Russia) and ByteDance / Pangle and Bigo Ads (China and Singapore). These transfers rely on the providers’ own safeguards, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. The full list of ad partners is on Appodeal’s partner page.

Security

Ad requests, gameplay statistics, and store requests use encrypted internet connections (HTTPS). Game progress stays on the device. We do not operate a player account server.

Retention

Local game data is kept until you uninstall the app or clear its data. There is no account to delete on our side.

Data already collected by Appodeal, ad networks, Unity, or Google is kept by those providers according to their own policies. Event data in Google Analytics for Firebase is kept for no longer than 14 months. Clearing the app does not erase copies they already hold. To delete gameplay statistics, use Settings → Privacy → Delete my data before you clear the app; for other data, use the contacts in the providers’ privacy policies.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to it, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal data on our own servers, most requests are handled through Settings → Privacy in the game (ad privacy choices, the statistics switch, and Delete my data), the Android advertising settings above, or directly with Appodeal, its partners, and Google — but you can always write to us and we will help.

You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.
