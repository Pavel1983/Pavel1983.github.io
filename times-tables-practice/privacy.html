Privacy Policy — Times Tables Practice

Last updated: 20 September 2026

This is the full privacy notice for Times Tables Practice. The same page is linked from the first-run screen in the app and is the URL we use on the Google Play store listing. The site is served over HTTPS.

This notice describes what the game stores, what starts after you tap Got it, and which third parties then process data. It is written in plain language so a parent or guardian can see the practices before ads and analytics start.

Who we are

This game is made by Pavel Totolin. Times Tables Practice is an educational multiplication-table app for children. This notice is for players in the Philippines (Data Privacy Act of 2012) and for Google Play.

We do not create an account. We do not ask for a name, email, or phone number.

What the game stores locally

On this device the game may keep:

  • quiz results, weak facts, progress, and achievements
  • language and sound preference

We do not send individual quiz answers or facts such as 7×8. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data deletes this local data.

What happens when you tap Got it

The first-run screen is a short notice, not parental consent under COPPA and not an account signup. It includes a button that opens this page.

Until you tap Got it, the game does not start analytics or ads.

If you tap Got it, analytics and advertising are enabled as described below. You can keep using the game without creating an account.

If you leave the app before tapping Got it, we do not start analytics or ads.

Analytics

After Got it, Unity Analytics collects:

  • a random identifier created by Unity to distinguish installations (not your name)
  • app version, language, and whether sound is on
  • which screen is open (menu, number_select, and similar)
  • session settings (mode, difficulty, timed, diagonal, selected tables as numbers like 1,2,5)
  • session summary: correct / wrong counts, duration, score, whether the timer ran out, new achievements

Unity’s SDK may also receive standard device fields (for example platform and a coarse country).

We use this to see how the game is used and to fix it. We do not use it to profile a child across other apps.

Unity’s own practices: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Advertising

After Got it, the app shows banner ads through Appodeal mediation. Appodeal and its demand partners (for example Google AdMob, Unity Ads, ironSource, and other networks enabled in the Appodeal dashboard) receive the technical data needed to request and fill a banner.

IP address

Ad and analytics requests travel over the internet. The device’s IP address is typically included in those requests so the SDK can reach Appodeal and ad networks, choose a suitable ad, and limit fraud. We do not use IP address to identify a child by name.

Advertising ID

We do not declare or request the Android Advertising ID (AAID) permission. Our Android builds strip com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID. We do not use AAID for remarketing or interest-based advertising.

Appodeal’s SDK documentation states that mediation may process IP addresses and mobile advertising identifiers. On our builds the OS is not granted the advertising-ID permission, so partners should not receive a usable AAID from this app. They may still receive other technical device data needed to fill a contextual, child-directed banner.

Location

We do not request location permission and we disable Appodeal’s location permission check. We do not collect precise GPS location. A coarse region may still be inferred from IP address by a network.

Child-directed ads

We configure the advertising SDK for child-directed use (setChildDirectedTreatment) before initialization. We do not request personalized or interest-based advertising. We do not use remarketing.

We do not send quiz answers, names, or emails to ad networks.

How Appodeal processes data, including IP address and advertising identifiers on their side: Appodeal Privacy Policy.

Google’s practices (including AdMob): https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children and child-directed use

This game is designed for children learning the times tables. We do not knowingly collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other information intended to directly contact a child.

Ads shown after Got it are requested as child-directed. We still do not treat the Got it tap as verified parental consent.

What we do not collect

  • name, email, phone, or login
  • individual quiz answers or facts
  • microphone, camera, contacts
  • precise GPS location
  • Android Advertising ID (we do not request or declare it)

Who receives data

After Got it, analytics and ads are processed by:

  • Unity (analytics) — privacy policy
  • Appodeal (mediation) — privacy policy
  • Google (AdMob and related services, when that network fills) — privacy policy
  • other demand sources enabled in the Appodeal dashboard, under their own policies

Those parties may process data on their own terms. This notice does not replace their policies. We link Appodeal’s policy here because they require publishers to disclose IP address and advertising-ID practices and to point players to Appodeal’s notice.

Where this notice appears

  • this HTTPS page: https://paveltotolin.dev/times-tables-practice/privacy.html
  • inside the app, from the first-run screen (opens this page)
  • the Google Play store listing privacy-policy field (same URL)

International processing

These providers may process data on servers located outside the Philippines, according to their own policies and applicable contractual and technical safeguards.

Security

Analytics and ad requests use ordinary encrypted internet connections (HTTPS). Local game progress stays on the device. We do not operate a player account server.

Retention

Local game data is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear app data. There is no account to delete on our side.

Data already collected by Unity, Appodeal, Google AdMob, or other advertising or analytics providers may remain with those providers under their own policies and procedures. Clearing the app does not erase copies they already hold.

Your privacy rights

You (or a parent / guardian) may contact us to ask about personal data processed for this app, request applicable rights under the Data Privacy Act, or ask for help with a privacy request.

You may also complain to the National Privacy Commission of the Philippines.

Uninstalling the app or clearing app data stops local collection on this device. It does not by itself delete data already held by Unity, Appodeal, or AdMob.
