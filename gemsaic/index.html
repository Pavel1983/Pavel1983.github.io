Gemsaic

A relaxing puzzle game: tap the stacks of jewels to send each gem to its place and fill pixel-art pictures from the bottom up. Plan your moves so the shelf never fills up.

  • Hand-made pixel-art levels with gentle difficulty
  • Daily Challenge: build a seasonal room piece by piece
  • Journal with every picture you have completed, sorted by theme
  • Boosters: Magnet, +Slot, and Peek
