Privacy Policy — Gemsaic

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This is the full privacy notice for Gemsaic, a puzzle game for Android in which you fill pixel-art pictures with jewels. This is the URL we use on the Google Play store listing. The site is served over HTTPS.

This notice describes what the game stores on your device, what data the advertising SDK collects, which third parties receive it, and what choices you have.

Who we are

Gemsaic is made by Pavel Totolin, an independent developer. This notice applies to anyone who plays the game, wherever they are.

The game has no account or login. We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number, and we do not run our own servers for the game.

What the game stores on your device

The game keeps its progress locally on the device:

  • unlocked levels, stars per level, and failed attempts (used to make a level gently easier)
  • booster counts (Magnet, +Slot, Peek)
  • Daily Challenge progress: collected room pieces, today’s tries, and an unfinished attempt
  • which pictures you have already seen in the Journal and which tutorials were shown
  • how many games you have finished (used to decide when an interstitial ad is due)

This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads: a banner at the bottom of the screen, interstitial ads between games, and optional rewarded videos (for an extra Daily Challenge try or a free booster). Ads are served through the Appodeal mediation SDK. Appodeal requests ads from its demand partners and ad networks (for example Google AdMob, AppLovin, Unity Ads, ironSource, Meta Audience Network, Mintegral, Liftoff (Vungle), BidMachine, and others) and shows the one that wins.

What the advertising SDK collects

To request, show, and measure ads, Appodeal and its partners may collect:

  • the device’s advertising ID (Google Advertising ID on Android)
  • the device’s IP address
  • device and app information: device model and manufacturer, operating system and version, language, screen size, network type and carrier, app name and version, time zone
  • a coarse location (country or region) derived from the IP address
  • ad interactions: which ads were requested, shown, clicked, or watched to the end, and ad revenue

This data is used to deliver ads, limit how often the same ad is shown, measure ad performance and attribution, prevent fraud, and — if you allow it — show personalized (interest-based) ads.

Appodeal’s SDK also includes optional measurement and diagnostics services (such as Adjust, AppsFlyer, Firebase, Meta, and Sentry). When they are active, they receive similar technical data about the device, the app, and ad events.

Location and permissions

The game does not request location permission and does not collect precise GPS location. A coarse region may be inferred from the IP address by Appodeal or ad networks. The game does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, or photos.

Consent in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the game shows a consent message (Google’s consent form, provided through Appodeal) before personalized ads. You can accept or refuse. If you refuse, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized, and ad partners are told not to use your data for profiling.

To change your choice later, clear the app’s data (Android Settings → Apps → Gemsaic → Storage → Clear data) and the consent message will be shown again. Note that this also deletes your game progress. You can also contact us by email.

Your advertising choices on Android

On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the device). After that, ad networks receive no advertising ID or are told not to personalize ads.

Third-party policies

  • Appodeal privacy policy: https://appodeal.com/privacy-policy/
  • Appodeal’s list of partners and their privacy policies: https://www.appodeal.com/home/partners-privacy-policies/
  • How Google uses data from apps that show its ads: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Game engine

Gemsaic is built with the Unity engine. The engine may send anonymous technical data to Unity, such as device model, operating system, graphics hardware, and performance or crash information, to help Unity improve the engine. Unity’s practices: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

The game itself does not use its own analytics service.

What we do not collect

  • name, email, phone number, or login
  • contacts, photos, camera, or microphone
  • precise GPS location
  • payment information (the game has no in-app purchases)

Who receives data

  • Appodeal (ad mediation) — privacy policy
  • ad networks and demand partners working through Appodeal, including Google AdMob — partner list
  • Unity (game engine) — privacy policy

These parties process data under their own policies. This notice does not replace their policies. We do not sell personal data for money. Sharing data with ad partners for interest-based advertising may count as “sharing” or “selling” under some laws (for example the California Consumer Privacy Act); you can stop it with the Android advertising settings described above.

Children

Gemsaic is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has used the game and data was collected, contact us and we will help.

International processing

Appodeal, ad networks, and Unity may process data on servers in countries other than the one where you play, under their own policies and applicable contractual and technical safeguards.

Security

Ad requests use encrypted internet connections (HTTPS). Game progress stays on the device. We do not operate a player account server.

Retention

Local game data is kept until you uninstall the app or clear its data. There is no account to delete on our side.

Data already collected by Appodeal, ad networks, or Unity is kept by those providers according to their own policies. Clearing the app does not erase copies they already hold; to ask them to delete it, use the contacts in their privacy policies.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to it, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal data on our own servers, most requests are handled through the advertising settings above or directly with Appodeal and its partners — but you can always write to us and we will help.

You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.
